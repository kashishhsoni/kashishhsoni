## Featured work

**B2B lead generation on LinkedIn for a banking technology company** (Consulting project)

I ran lead generation and awareness for six months across the UK, the Middle East and India on $15,947.71 of spend. The top three campaigns delivered 67 leads at $37.11 per lead, the best UK campaign reached $34.94, and the account produced 125 leads in total. Analysis showed a 10x cost per lead gap between two creatives promoting the same whitepaper. [Read the case study](https://github.com/kashishhsoni/marketing-case-studies#case-study-1)

**Event registrations and leads for a business software conference** (In-house at a global business software company)

I ran Meta and LinkedIn campaigns for a two-day conference and 11 industry tracks on $133,701 of spend. They produced 22,289 registrations and 10,704 leads, at $9.58 per lead for the main event. Analysis showed that the cheapest registrations did not always become the cheapest leads, which changed how I judge event campaigns. [Read the case study](https://github.com/kashishhsoni/marketing-case-studies#case-study-2)

**Google Ads search in India** (In-house at a global business software company, with the paid search team)

I looked after the India search campaigns with the team, including bid and keyword adjustments. From January to November 2026 the account produced 84,358 leads at $3.11 each from $262,001 of spend, with a 20.7% click to lead rate.
